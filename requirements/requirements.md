# Music Festival Scheduler - Requirements

## Current Features

1. The app displays the lineup of the selected festival, organized by day once set times are published (Mad Cool 2026: Wed July 8 - Sat July 11).
2. Users can filter the lineup by day using a day selector at the top of the Lineup screen.
3. Each show card displays the artist name, description, set time (start and end), stage name, and stage location; unpublished set times and stages show as "TBA".
4. Users can add a show to their personal schedule by tapping the + button on a show card.
5. Users can remove a show from their schedule by tapping the checkmark on a show card (Lineup screen) or the X button (Schedule screen).
6. The My Schedule screen displays the selected festival's saved shows grouped by day with stage and location details; shows without a set time are grouped under "Set Time TBA".
7. All data is stored locally in an embedded SQLite database — no server or internet connection required.
8. Mad Cool 2026 includes 5 real festival stages: Region of Madrid, Orange, The Loop Iberdrola, Mahou Cinco Estrellas, and Mahou Reserva.
9. Mad Cool 2026 is seeded with 83 shows across 4 days with exact set times from the official Mad Cool schedule.
10. The app uses a dark theme with purple accent colors.
11. Each show card includes a "Listen on YouTube Music" button that opens the artist in YouTube Music on the user's phone.
12. The app includes a Map tab showing the selected festival's official map (Mad Cool 2026) so users can navigate the venue without needing internet, or a placeholder when no map has been published.
13. The app bundles multiple festivals (Mad Cool 2026 and Primavera Sound 2027). Users switch festivals from the header; the most recently added festival is selected on launch.
14. Primavera Sound 2027 (June 2-6, 2027, Parc del Fòrum, Barcelona) is seeded with its 152 announced artists scraped from primaverasound.com; stages and set times are TBA.
15. Adding a festival never removes previous festivals or the user's saved shows.
